FAQ

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Just send me a message.

How much does a couple shoot in Haarlem cost?

A couple shoot with Dunelights Photography starts at €149. You get 60–90 minutes of shooting at golden hour and at least 30 edited photos in an online gallery.

Where do the shoots take place?

Favourite spots are the beaches and dunes of Zandvoort and Bloemendaal, and the centre of Haarlem. Amsterdam and other locations are possible too. For events, I come to you.

When will I receive my photos?

Usually within 7 days, in an online gallery where you can download all photos in high quality. For events, within 10 days.

I'm not comfortable in front of the camera. Is that a problem?

Not at all, most people aren't. I keep shoots relaxed, give simple directions and mostly let you talk, walk and laugh. That's where the best photos come from.

What if the weather is bad?

Then we simply pick a new date, free of charge. A bit of cloud often gives beautiful soft light, though.

Still have a question?

Ask me directly. Your question goes straight to my inbox and I'll reply by email.
